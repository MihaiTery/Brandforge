# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Service providers, broadly: anyone offering a service who needs a stronger presence on social media or a better website but doesn't have in-house production. This is the lead audience the site speaks to first. The wider circle around them — small brands, online shops, solo creators, consultants, personal projects — is real (named explicitly in llms.txt) but secondary to the service-provider framing.

## Product Purpose

BrandForge is a digital studio built around two services: social media management (strategy, creative direction, content and consistent posting across Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube) and custom website building (from a brand presentation page to an online shop, fast and SEO-optimized). The site's job is to read as a real, already-operating studio with verifiable proof, not a pitch deck: the social accounts it manages and the websites it built are shown directly, not described in the abstract.

There's no online checkout on this site. Every path funnels toward a WhatsApp conversation, where scope and timeline (including urgent cases) get worked out directly.

## Positioning

Two fires, one workshop: brands can hire BrandForge for social media management, website building, or both, under one creative direction — with proof on the page for each.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: WhatsApp contact ("Hai să vorbim despre brandul tău") — present throughout the page (hero, floating action button, services, website portfolio, contact).
- Secondary CTAs: contextual WhatsApp variants with pre-filled messages tailored to what the visitor was just looking at (general, social media, new website, urgent website).
- The line a visitor should remember after 10 seconds: BrandForge runs the social media and builds the website, and both are provable on this page, not just claimed.
- Proof on hand:
  - Website portfolio (nebo.ro, florianmolea.ro, realityexe.com, nebi.ro): live-site screenshots plus real Google Lighthouse scores (performance, accessibility, best practices, SEO), checked periodically and shown as evidence, not just promised.
  - Social showcase: the accounts BrandForge actively manages (Florian Molea Instructor, InventorPath, OPS Founder), linked out to Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube.
- Urgent delivery is a real differentiator: standard turnaround is a few business days, but urgent website projects can be delivered in up to 24h in special cases — stated plainly in the services panel, the website portfolio panel, and the FAQ, never oversold as the default.

## Brand Personality

Bold, intense, alive. The molten-forge visual system (obsidian backdrop, WebGL lava shader, ember and smoke motion, "fire" language throughout the copy) is the personality made literal, not decoration on top of a neutral brand. Confident and physical rather than corporate — the same voice now carries a portfolio-and-proof site instead of a single-product funnel.

## Anti-references

Must not read as a template-shop or Fiverr-style gig service. The portfolio (real screenshots, real Lighthouse scores, real links) and the live-managed social accounts are what argue against that reading — they replace the old "1€ reel, no-risk hook" mechanic as the site's credibility device.

## Design Principles

- Show, don't promise. Every claim on the page (site speed, SEO, social growth) should point at something the visitor can click and verify — a live site, a live account, a real score.
- Urgent delivery is a special case, not the headline. State it clearly (up to 24h) without implying it's the normal timeline for every project.
- Direction, not just output. Every surface should keep signaling that human creative direction stands behind both services — the brand's actual differentiator against a template shop or a generic freelancer.
- Contact stays effortless. WhatsApp is the only conversion path; every CTA should lower the friction to starting that conversation, with a message pre-filled for the context the visitor was in.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA is the bar. No stricter requirement or specific user need beyond that was flagged. This is already the site's practical baseline: verified contrast ratios, visible focus states, and full `prefers-reduced-motion` support are implemented and were audited into place, not aspirational.

# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Service providers, broadly: anyone offering a service who needs a stronger presence on social media or a better website but doesn't have in-house production. This is the lead audience the site speaks to first. The wider circle around them — small brands, online shops, solo creators, consultants, personal projects — is real (named explicitly in llms.txt) but secondary to the service-provider framing.

## Product Purpose

BrandForge is a digital studio built around two priced services: social media management, billed from €1 per reel posted (strategy, creative direction, content and consistent posting across Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube), and custom website building, sold as a €20/month subscription that bundles the build itself with ongoing hosting and maintenance for as long as the client stays subscribed. The site's job is to read as a real, already-operating studio with verifiable proof, not a pitch deck: the social accounts it manages and the websites it built are shown directly, not described in the abstract, and the prices themselves are stated plainly rather than hidden behind a "contact us" wall.

There's no online checkout or live billing on this site. Prices are advertised plainly (starting-from framing), but every path still funnels toward a WhatsApp conversation, where the exact scope, the subscription start date, and payment are worked out directly.

## Positioning

Two fires, one workshop: brands can hire BrandForge for social media management (from €1/reel posted), website building (from €20/month, all-inclusive), or both, under one creative direction — with proof on the page for each, and the price stated up front instead of hidden.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: WhatsApp contact ("Hai să vorbim despre brandul tău") — present throughout the page (hero, floating action button, services, pricing, website portfolio, contact).
- Secondary CTAs: contextual WhatsApp variants with pre-filled messages tailored to what the visitor was just looking at (general, social media, new website, urgent website).
- The line a visitor should remember after 10 seconds: BrandForge runs the social media from €1 per reel posted and builds the website for €20/month all-inclusive, and both are provable on this page, not just claimed.
- A dedicated pricing panel (`#preturi`, right after the services panel) is the one place both numbers live side by side, each with what's included and its own WhatsApp CTA — this is the panel to point to when the ask is specifically "show me the price."
- Proof on hand:
  - Website portfolio (nebo.ro, florianmolea.ro, realityexe.com, nebi.ro, inventorpath.eu, citydock.ro): live-site screenshots plus real Google Lighthouse scores (performance, accessibility, best practices, SEO), checked periodically and shown as evidence, not just promised. The homepage panel is a compact teaser (six mini cards, no scores); the dedicated portfolio.html page carries the full case study — bigger screenshot, description, and complete score breakdown — per site, and is the link to share when the portfolio itself is the point.
  - Social showcase: the accounts BrandForge actively manages (Florian Molea Instructor, InventorPath, OPS Founder), linked out to Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube.
- Urgent delivery is a real differentiator: standard turnaround is a few business days, but urgent website projects can be delivered in up to 24h in special cases — stated plainly in the services panel, the pricing panel, the website portfolio panel, and the FAQ, never oversold as the default.

## Brand Personality

Bold, intense, alive. The molten-forge visual system (obsidian backdrop, WebGL lava shader, ember and smoke motion, "fire" language throughout the copy) is the personality made literal, not decoration on top of a neutral brand. Confident and physical rather than corporate — the same voice now carries a portfolio-and-proof site instead of a single-product funnel.

## Anti-references

Must not read as a template-shop or Fiverr-style gig service, despite the low entry prices (€1/reel, €20/month). The portfolio (real screenshots, real Lighthouse scores, real links) and the live-managed social accounts are what argue against that reading — clear pricing is meant to land as transparency and confidence, never as a signal of low quality or a discount-bin service.

## Design Principles

- Show, don't promise. Every claim on the page (site speed, SEO, social growth, price) should point at something the visitor can click and verify — a live site, a live account, a real score, a real number.
- Price the entry points as transparency, not a discount. €1/reel and €20/month should read as "here's exactly what this costs," never as "this is cheap work." Production values, copy, and craft should all argue against the price being read as "you get what you pay for."
- Urgent delivery is a special case, not the headline. State it clearly (up to 24h) without implying it's the normal timeline for every project.
- Direction, not just output. Every surface should keep signaling that human creative direction stands behind both services — the brand's actual differentiator against a template shop or a generic freelancer.
- Contact stays effortless. WhatsApp is the only conversion path; every CTA should lower the friction to starting that conversation, with a message pre-filled for the context the visitor was in.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA is the bar. No stricter requirement or specific user need beyond that was flagged. This is already the site's practical baseline: verified contrast ratios, visible focus states, and full `prefers-reduced-motion` support are implemented and were audited into place, not aspirational.
